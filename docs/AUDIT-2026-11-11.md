# Tagkin: system and journey audit (11 Nov 2026)

**Status 11 Nov 2026:** all critical and high design findings are fixed (see ROADMAP.md, Audit fixes). Still open: A3 (confirm products, Stage C), E2 (retention, Stage C), E4 (buy domain by 15 Nov), E5 (file trademark, Stage E).

An owner's audit of the whole plan: business model, rules, journeys and operations. Each finding has a severity, the fix, and whether it needs Selasey's decision. Accepted fixes move into BUSINESS-RULES.md and ROADMAP.md.

Severity: **Critical** (breaks the business or a promise), **High** (users get stuck or harmed), **Medium** (friction or cost), **Low** (polish).

## A. Business model and money

| # | Finding | Severity | Fix | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **The free home-print tag competes with paid tags and hurts quality.** Paper tags fade and tear; a finder who can't scan a soaked paper tag blames Tagkin. | High | Make the free tag the **phone lock-screen tag** only. Remove home printing. Every printed tag is then bought, and every printed tag meets our quality. | Yes |
| A2 | **Single tags plus €2.50 shipping feel expensive** (one sticker = €7.40 before the offer). | Medium | Lead with packs (3 stickers), and free shipping over a threshold (e.g. €15). | Yes |
| A3 | **Product range may not be printable on demand.** Engraved metal pet tags and acrylic keychains with our exact sizes are not standard print-on-demand items. | High | Confirm with print partners in Stage C before promising shapes and prices. Launch with what a partner can make. | Stage C |
| A4 | **Shop sales are a different business** (packaging, barcodes, stock, retailer margins, returns). | Medium | Keep shops for later. Launch online only. | Already "later" |
| A5 | **Copies of one tag earn per copy**, which is right, but copies and separate tags look alike in checkout. | Low | Checkout line says "2 copies of TK-7Q4M-9XRA". | No |

## B. Ownership, security and trust

| # | Finding | Severity | Fix | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Anyone holding an unclaimed tag can activate it.** A gift lost in the post, or a shop pack opened in store, can be claimed by a stranger. | Critical | Unclaimed tags need an **activation PIN** that is not on the tag: on the gift card inside the box, or under a scratch panel on shop packaging. | Yes |
| B2 | **The owner's phone is the remote control.** If the lost item is the phone itself (lock-screen tag), the owner can't open the app to mark it lost or reply. | Critical | Owners must be able to sign in at **tagkin.app** on any device: Lost mode, messages, contacts. | Yes |
| B3 | **No account recovery.** Sign-in is email only; losing access to that email means losing every tag. | High | Add a recovery phone or second email at set-up, and a support recovery process. | Yes |
| B4 | **Tags don't stop theft.** A thief removes the sticker. Security positioning must not imply anti-theft. | High (overpromise) | Copy talks about protection through return and proof of ownership, never "theft-proof". Add to the copy rules. | Yes |
| B5 | **Finder page can reveal too much**: a photo taken at home, a pet's medical needs, a full name. | Medium | Photo guidance at upload ("no faces, house numbers or addresses"), first name only for pets, care notes behind a tap (done). | No |
| B6 | **Verified handover is listed as Premium**, but fake "that's mine" claims at desks are a safety issue. | Medium | Offer a basic handover code free; keep advanced proof (vault) for Premium. | Yes |
| B7 | **Codes are case- and dash-insensitive, and lookups are rate-limited** (done). Lookup must also never confirm whether a code exists for retired tags beyond "not active". | Low | Already designed. | No |

## C. Finder journey

| # | Finding | Severity | Fix | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **If the owner never replies, the finder is left waiting.** | High | After a set time with no reply: the backup contact is alerted, and the finder page suggests handing the item to staff or a lost-and-found desk. | No |
| C2 | **Anonymous finders only see replies while the page is open.** | Medium | Offer optional browser notifications on the thank-you page (no account, no email). | No |
| C3 | **The finder thank-you offer** (first tag €2 off) needs a way to reach them later if they leave no email. | Low | Show a short code on the thank-you page ("THANKS2"). | No |

## D. Owner journey

| # | Finding | Severity | Fix | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **The second tag has no correct path.** Once the free tag is used, "+ New" leads to "Your free tag is in use", whose button opens ordering for the existing backpack tag. | Critical | New tags after the free one: Create → type → details → message → customise → **order and pay → tag becomes live when the order is placed**. | No |
| D2 | **The free tag shows colours and shapes** it can't have (free = plain). | Medium | Solved by A1 (free tag = lock screen). Otherwise show "plain" only for the free tag. | With A1 |
| D3 | **"Tag is live" before a tag is ordered** suggests a sticker exists. | Medium | For ordered tags: "Your tag is ready. It works as soon as it arrives." The code is reserved and the finder page is live from order. | No |
| D4 | **Lock-screen tag mixed into physical customise** caused the sticker swap (now removed). | Done | Lock screen is its own digital product: same set-up, its own preview, "Set as lock screen", never shipped. | Done |
| D5 | **Gift a tag hidden in Orders** and previously inside ordering a set-up tag. | Done | Create a tag now offers **For myself / Gift a tag**. Gifts are blank tags of a chosen product and colour; the recipient picks the belonging. | Done |
| D6 | **Product shape vs tag type.** A gifted luggage label could be set up as an item tag. | Low | Allowed. Product shape suggests the type; the recipient chooses the type at set-up, then it's locked. | No |
| D7 | **Onboarding asks for notifications before the user has a tag.** | Low | Ask after the first tag is live, when the value is clear. | No |

## E. Operations and promises

| # | Finding | Severity | Fix | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **"Reports reviewed within 24 hours"** and **"We reply within one working day"** are promises a solo founder can't always keep. | High (overpromise) | "We review every report" and "We aim to reply within two working days". | Yes |
| E2 | **Message retention period** is still undecided, but the UI mentions it. | Medium | Decide in Stage C (suggest 90 days after a return). | Stage C |
| E3 | **Translation and SMS costs grow with usage** before income does. | Medium | Cache translations of owner messages; SMS only later. | No |
| E4 | **tagkin.app is printed on every tag but not yet owned.** Publishing the design publicly makes the name visible. | Critical | Buy the domain **before** publishing to Figma Community. | Yes |
| E5 | **Trademark searched, not filed.** | High | File the EU trademark before launch (budget needed). | Yes |

## G. Added 11 Nov 2026 (found by Selasey)

| # | Finding | Severity | Fix | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **No step to choose how to pay.** Checkout only said payment was "handled by our print partner", which was also wrong for print-on-demand (Tagkin is the seller). Earlier audits treated payment as a provider's black box instead of a user step. | High | Pay with: Apple Pay or Google Pay, card, PayPal, Klarna, via Stripe, on both order and gift checkout; button names the method. | Done |
| G2 | **Leftover "print it yourself" copy** on Create a tag and the website after home printing was removed. | Medium | Rewritten. Future rule changes get a text search across all screens. | Done |

Lesson for later audits: walk every journey as the user, including the moments a provider handles (payment, email delivery, maps), and search all copy whenever a rule changes.

## F. What is right and should stay

- Privacy model (relay, hidden contacts, city-level location).
- One code, one belonging; codes never reused.
- Bought tags free for life; no subscription at launch.
- Helper wording for finders; never "stolen".
- Business rules, brief and roadmap as separate, linked documents.
